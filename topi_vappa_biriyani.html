Our Story

Crafted by Passion,
Inspired by Tradition

Born out of the combined experience and love for biriyani from six brothers, Topi Vappa Biriyani brings the rich flavours of Chennai-style biriyani to the fast-casual dining scene across South India.

With a dedication to authenticity and quality, Topi Vappa uses only the freshest ingredients and time-honoured cooking techniques — from slow-dum biriyani to live-fire Al Faham BBQ — to deliver an exceptional dining experience every single time.

What started in Trichy has now grown to 35+ outlets across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and the UAE — with each outlet maintaining the same home-cooked standard that made the first outlet legendary.
